01THE STORY

NOT A NEW LEGEND.
JUST MY VERSION OF V.

I've followed Cyberpunk since long before release. Over many playthroughs I completed the story, explored every corner of Night City, experimented with builds and started finding my own pace in its world.

Serafim Veyr was shaped gradually: her face, red hair, cyberware, clothing and even the little rituals of her daily life. I don't rush every quest. Some days she simply walks, takes the metro, visits home and changes her outfit.

I originally made her only for myself. The thought of sharing her grew from curiosity — and from appreciation for the mod authors whose work makes this possible.

05FROM ROLEPLAY TO ORIGINAL FICTION

ONE WORLD
INSPIRED ANOTHER.

CYBERPUNK 2077 → AXIOM

Playing and roleplaying Serafim influenced the way I imagined a separate original character in my own fictional universe, AXIOM. Some of the attitude and visual ideas survived that transition, but these are two distinct characters belonging to different worlds.

This fan archive keeps their origins clear. AXIOM is an independent personal creative and technical project — not an official Cyberpunk 2077 continuation or a CD Projekt RED product.
